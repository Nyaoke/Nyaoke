# Interactions (extracted from live site)

## Nav
Sticky top. Near-transparent over hero, becomes solid white with subtle bottom
border / light blur after scrolling past the hero. Center links, right Contact pill.
Mobile collapses to a menu toggle opening a full-screen sheet.

## Hero
Left-aligned H1 in a centered container. Availability pill with green dot above.
Two CTAs: black pill primary, text stat secondary ("99+ Happy clients").

## Logo marquee
Seamless infinite translateX loop, constant speed, set duplicated for seamless
wrap. No pause on hover. Reused under "Trusted by many".

## Latest projects
Two-column card grid. Rounded image thumbnail (16px). Title + subtitle + "View
Project". On hover the image scales subtly and the title nudges.

## Testimonials
Static three-column grid of cards with circular colored avatars.

## FAQ
Numbered accordion (01..05). Single item open at a time. Smooth height animation,
plus/indicator rotates on open.

## Section reveals
Elements fade and rise ~12px once on entering viewport, light stagger on grouped
items. Disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.

## Footer
Black background. Large stacked wordmark: "Lets / design / build / create /
incredible work together." where the verbs cycle/animate. Oversized "NYAOKE"
wordmark spanning the very bottom. Copyright reads "© 2025 Ted Nyaoke" on live.

## Counters
"99+ Happy clients" is static text, not an animated counter.
